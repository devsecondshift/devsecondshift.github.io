The game

A stream chat tells Tyoma to go get a factory job, and he wakes up in 1975 at the Mayatnik plant. Turn, saw and assemble by hand on real machines, fill orders for the people of Berestov and restore a rusty workshop. Save up, build a time machine and go home. Or stay.

Berestov, 2026. Tyoma runs a night stream from the abandoned Mayatnik plant. The chat writes "go get a factory job", the tower clock that stopped in 1962 strikes, and in the morning he wakes up in the same hall, in 1975. The plant is running, the foreman needs an apprentice, and the phone still catches the stream.

Earn your pay at the machines, put money aside and build a time machine from the blueprints of the 1962 tower clock.

Real machines, your hands

  • The chisel follows your mouse: shavings curl off, and a rolling pin or a baluster grows out of a wooden bar.
  • Measure with a tape, mark with a pencil, cut along your own line, drill, sand, and assemble with glue, clamps and screws.
  • Every part gets measured: 48.3 mm against 48 ± 0.5.
  • No timers while you work and no game over. Spoil a part and you make it again.
  • From wood to metal: later chapters open the metal lathe, the forge and welding.

Orders with stories

  • Townspeople come to the counter: a milking stool for the cowshed, toy blocks for a grandson's birthday, a birch rolling pin, a bread board for the canteen.
  • Each order is a sheet with terms: sizes, wood, quality and the day it is due. Run late and you get less pay, but you keep the order.
  • Regulars come back with new requests, and their stories carry on from chapter to chapter.

From under the tarp to a shine

  • Machines wait under tarps, rusty and full of cobwebs. Pull the tarp off, restore the machine and put it to work.
  • You see every upgrade on the machine itself: a stop on the saw, a chuck on the lathe, a jig on the bench.
  • The hall changes chapter by chapter: the boarded windows and the junk go, flowers turn up on the sills.

From apprentice to master

  • Ranks and a perk tree with three branches: Hands, Eye and Head.
  • Each machine has its own skill with five levels, from a crooked first cut to a clean pass.
  • 8 chapters: from a stool with three parts to a tower clock with 55.

The chat from 2026

  • Your phone still catches the stream. The chat watches your shifts, jokes, gives advice and asks when you are coming back. Mom writes too.

The time machine fund

  • Every evening you decide how much of the day's pay goes into the fund. Money in the fund stays there.
  • The machine hides in the blueprints of the tower clock. Build it with your own hands, then choose: go home or stay in 1975.

After the story, the workshop stays yours in an endless free mode.

The developer

Second Shift is Mikhail, a solo developer who makes ZAVOD after his day job.

The machines follow how real woodturners and carpenters work. Woodturners on Reddit looked at the lathe, and their notes went into the game.

AI disclosure

We generated the sound effects and music in ElevenLabs and picked them by hand. We drafted the game texts and the English translation with Claude and edited them ourselves. Our Python scripts in Blender build the 3D models; we use no image or 3D generators.
